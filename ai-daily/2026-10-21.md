### A Controlled LLM Workflow for Evidence-Grounded Diagnosis of Risk Score Differences

- 原文：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23962>
- 发布日期：2026-10-20

来源摘要（保留原文）：

> arXiv:2610.23962v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We propose a controlled Large Language Model (LLM) workflow for explaining risk score differences using similar historical transactions. The workflow combines reference retrieval and contrastive SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP) evidence with reference-based LLM reasoning and deterministic validation. Experiments on a test dataset show that the workflow produces more consistent, evidence-grounded and traceable diagnoses than the same LLM without structured workflow guidance.
